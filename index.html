Privacy Policy for Sonory

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Overview

Sonory respects your privacy. This application does not require user accounts and does not directly collect personally identifiable information.

In-App Purchases

Sonory offers optional premium purchases through Apple App Store billing. Payments and subscription management are handled directly by Apple.

Sonory uses RevenueCat, a third-party service, to securely manage subscription and purchase status across devices.

RevenueCat may process anonymous purchase-related identifiers necessary for validating subscriptions and restoring purchases.

Data Collection

Sonory does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, messages, photos, or precise location data.

Sonory does not use advertising trackers.

Accounts

Sonory does not require account creation or login.

Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store (payments and subscriptions)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
